Ledger Viewer for FreeAgent
A private, offline, read-only viewer for your FreeAgent data export
1
Export your data
In FreeAgent, go to Settings → Import/Export Data and download your full data export (.xlsx), plus your attachments folder if you want receipts and documents linked too.
2
Upload it below
Everything is processed right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server — this page works fully offline once it's loaded.
3
Save your own copy
Once loaded, use "Download self-contained copy" in the sidebar to save a personal file with your data baked in, so you don't need to re-upload next time.
